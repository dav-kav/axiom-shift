WEBSITE DESIGN PROPOSAL
Small Business Package

Axiom Shift

Prepared: August 24, 2026


1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

This proposal covers the design, development, and launch of a custom small business website including up to 10 mock-up reviews, domain reservation, and 12 months of managed hosting.

Scope: Up to 8 pages (e.g., Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Blog, Contact)
Timeline: 4-6 weeks from discovery to launch
Technology: Static HTML/CSS/JS or WordPress CMS - chosen based on client needs


2. PRICING MODEL - COST PLUS

This is a cost-plus arrangement with no markup on labor or direct costs. The friends and family rate reflects David's direct relationship with Axiom Shift - labor is billed at actual cost, not retail.


3. PROJECT SCOPE - 25 HOURS ESTIMATED

This estimate is based on actual tracked time from the Axiom Shift website build (May 2026), adjusted upward to account for the full 10-review mock-up cycle required in this scope.

Actual baseline: Axiom Shift single-page site = ~12 hours tracked
This proposal: 8-page site with discovery, full design review cycle, and domain/hosting = 25 hours


PHASE 1 - Discovery & Strategy (3 hours)

- Business goals and audience mapping
- Content strategy and site architecture (sitemap)
- Design brief and creative direction

Hours: 3


PHASE 2 - Design Phase: Mock-Up Reviews (12 hours)

This is the core deliverable. The client receives 5 to 10 design reviews across the following rounds:

  Round 1 (2-3 concepts) - Initial creative directions
  Round 2 (2 refinements) - Feedback incorporated
  Round 3 (2 refinements) - Final polish pass
  Round 4 (2 final reviews) - Desktop and mobile responsive layouts
  Round 5 (1-2 reviews) - Approved design locked

Note: Additional design rounds beyond 10 reviews billed at $250/hour.

Hours: 12


PHASE 3 - Development (7 hours)

- Front-end build (HTML/CSS/JS or CMS)
- Content population and branding
- SEO foundation (meta tags, sitemap, structured data)
- Cross-browser and mobile testing

Hours: 7


PHASE 4 - Launch (3 hours)

- Domain reservation and DNS configuration
- SSL/HTTPS setup
- Managed hosting setup (12 months included)
- Final QA and go-live

Hours: 3


HOURS SUMMARY

  Phase 1 - Discovery & Strategy:   3 hours
  Phase 2 - Design Reviews:       12 hours
  Phase 3 - Development:            7 hours
  Phase 4 - Launch:                 3 hours
  TOTAL LABOR:                    25 hours
