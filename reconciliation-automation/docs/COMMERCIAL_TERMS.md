# Commercial Terms — Bank Reconciliation Automation
**Wander Boutique Hotel** | Axiom Shift
**Date:** August 3, 2026

---

## 1. Service Description

Axiom Shift provides an automated bank reconciliation service for Wander Boutique Hotel ("Client"), consisting of:

- A Google Apps Script that reads BAC bank CSV exports, classifies transactions automatically, and produces a QuickBooks-ready upload file
- Ongoing maintenance of classification rules as account activity patterns evolve
- Support for questions and troubleshooting during normal business hours

The service is delivered as a hosted Google Apps Script under Axiom Shift's Google account. Client authorizes the script to access their Google Sheet. The script itself remains under Axiom Shift's control and can be updated or deactivated by Axiom Shift at any time.
